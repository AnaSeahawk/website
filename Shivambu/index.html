Shivambu

The body’s own waters, in everyday practice and long history.

Shivambu is a name for the practice of working with one’s own urine: the body’s own waters. Here it is an everyday practice, met with curiosity and read alongside its long history in medicine, religion and alchemy.

The writing begins with the story of a beginning. Around it gather Circles in The Living Year, conversations one to one, and the research of Sovereign Biophysics.
