Distillation and the alchemical roots,told in pictures.

THE CIRCLE

Live by video.
Two hours: 90 minutes for the story in pictures, then 30 minutes of open conversation.

The body is a distillery before it has a word for distillation.

It separates, concentrates, transforms and returns. The kidneys filter, the gut ferments, the skin salts its surface, the womb prepares, releases and begins again. A still invents none of this. It gives the process a visible edge.

The Living Waters follows that edge through a year of distilling the body’s own waters at home. From a single material, two waters rise: first a sharp spirit, then a clear and quiet essence. The third stays behind in the chamber: the retentate, dense and mineral. The old alchemists had names for all three. Their working names here are the Solar spirit, the Lunar essence and the Saturn serum.

What the story holds

  • ☿
    The first vessel

    The womb and the water of the womb: the body as the first distillery, long before any glass.

  • ☽
    The cycle as living alchemy

    Release, clearing, brightening, deepening. The menstrual cycle beside the four alchemical stages of nigredo, albedo, citrinitas and rubedo, held as correspondence and felt in the body.

  • ⚱
    The women before us

    Tapputi-Belatekallim, the perfumer of Babylon. Mary the Jewess and the gentle water bath that still carries her name. Cleopatra the Alchemist and the serpent that swallows its tail. And the unnamed women of kitchens, births, herbs and fire.

  • ≋
    The still and its three waters

    What happens at the still, and how each fraction behaves: the Solar spirit, the Lunar essence, the Saturn serum.

  • ✦
    A map between languages

    Alchemy, physiology and lived experience side by side: vessel, fire, spirit, carrier, salt and return.

  • ☉
    The year as the measure

    One run gives a reading. A month gives a pattern. A year gives the range. The story ends where The Living Year begins.

The labels carry no year: the water was for use, never for keeping. The photograph’s date gives the year, 2026, and the sky gives the rest.

The Sun alone holds the distilling to a single day. The Moon, moving a degree in about two hours, narrows it to under two hours of that day. How the sky dates a page, or a jar, is the subject of Keeping the Year.

Taking part

The story suits anyone already cycling their waters, and anyone drawn to the still, to alchemy, or to the old knowledge of women. Nothing needs to be prepared or brought, beyond a notebook and a question.

After the pictures, the Circle opens for half an hour or more. It is a time to share what the story stirred, as much as to ask about it. What part felt familiar? Where has the body already known separation and return? The conversation moves around the whole circle, among everyone there.

Joining

The Living Waters comes in the first lunation of The Living Year, after the new moon of 6 April 2027, once Keeping the Year has opened the season at the spring gate. Its date, its cost and its number of seats are listed at least one week ahead, here and in the new-moon letter, and seats are booked one at a time.
