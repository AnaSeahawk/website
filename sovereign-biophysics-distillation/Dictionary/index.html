Dictionary

A reference for the terms, measurements, and traditions that recur in Sovereign Biophysics.

Distillation

The liquid collected from the still after the vapour condenses is the distillate. Some records describe the whole distillate. Others follow the fractions collected separately.

Spirit
The first fraction collected. In the alchemical language of the archive, solar describes its volatile rise.
Essence
The clear middle fraction. Lunar describes the quiet, carrying quality of this water.
Serum
The liquid that remains in the still, also called the retentate. Saturnian describes its fixed character.
