Before and After the Still

Three records from June 2026.

June 18, 2026

Two Samples from One Day

Fresh urine samples from the same person, before distillation.

The midday sample read nearly four times as high in conductivity as the morning sample. The pH moved by a tenth.

When the samples were measured

The morning sample was collected around 6–7 a.m. and measured around 1 p.m. The midday sample was measured within minutes of collection.

How much of the difference belonged to the hour of collection, and how much to the time the sample had been sitting? The day’s material was later blended, so the comparison ends with the two readings before distillation.

If the starting material can vary this much, what carries through the still, and what changes?

June 10, 2026

Two Fresh Batches Through the Still

Two separate batches of fresh urine were measured before and after distillation. The second batch started at more than twice the conductivity of the first.

In both batches, the pH rose and the conductivity fell. The two distillates read close together on those measures. The dissolved hydrogen readings were further apart, at 58 and 152 parts per billion.

The third run began with material that had already been distilled. The available readings cover the input and output of that second pass.

What would repeat if two runs began with the same material?

June 20, 2026

Two Runs from the Same Blend

The urine blend had aged for at least a month. Two portions from it were distilled separately.

The spirit is the first fraction collected, and the essence is the second. The liquid that remains in the still is the serum, or retentate. Their alchemical qualities are solar for the spirit, lunar for the essence, and Saturnian for the serum.

The spirit readings were close in pH, oxidation-reduction potential, and dissolved hydrogen. In the essence, oxidation-reduction potential was the same in both runs, while conductivity differed by more than twofold.

The serum was measured in Run B only.

Would these readings stay as close across two runs from another aged blend?
