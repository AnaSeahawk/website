The former inline darkfield vesicle emblem was removed. Its replacement is an
original hand-painted study developed for this page: a restrained blue field,
one radiant centre, broad nested forms, and a clean axial composition informed
by Indian Neo-Tantric modernism and Hilma af Klint's symbolic abstraction. It
does not reproduce an existing artwork or claim to document the observed
phenomenon.

The same self-contained dark-indigo plate is used in both site themes. It was
checked in light and dark modes at desktop and phone widths before release; a
second theme-specific rendering was not needed.

The original caption fragment describing literal packets of vesicles was later
replaced with Reich's retrospective line from *The Cancer Biopathy*, Ch. VII.
A brief note identifies the plate as an imagining of the blue he described,
rather than a photograph.
