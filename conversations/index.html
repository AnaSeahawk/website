CIRCLES

By video.
Two hours: 90 minutes on the subject, then 30 minutes of open conversation.

A Circle is a gathering around one subject, held through The Living Year by the Sun’s seasons.

Every voice counts. A Circle begins with its subject and opens into conversation. Each person speaks from their own experience, and the others listen with care. What one brings meets what another brings, and in that meeting the Circle comes alive.

A seat can be taken in two ways: a speaking seat, to join the conversation, or a listening seat, at a lower cost, to be present and receive. Each Circle’s date, seats and costs are listed at least one week ahead, and seats are booked one at a time.

A Circle for your group

A Circle just for a community, a group of friends or an organisation, on one of these subjects or something else. It is shaped together, and the cost is agreed together. In person is possible too, when a host invites Ana and holds the space: a yoga studio, an eco project, a community that gathers the group.

A free 15‑minute call to plan it is welcome; just ask in the email.

CONVERSATIONS

One to one, by video.
20, 60 or 90 minutes.

A conversation is time alone with Ana, for the questions, observations and curiosity that grow around a practice, a home or a way of living.

It draws on lived practice, sustained observation and years of independent study, from herbalism into Ayurveda. The subject comes from the person booking, and the way in is found together. Any Circle can be taken alone this way, in 90 minutes.
