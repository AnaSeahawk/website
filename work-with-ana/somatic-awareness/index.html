Attention from within.A 90-minute workshop exploring body, movement, play, and habitat.

THE WORKSHOP

90 minutes, by video.
One to one, or for your group.

There is a difference between moving through an exercise and noticing yourself as you move. This workshop invites you to explore that difference through gentle movement, curiosity, and play.

Together, we’ll explore balance, breath, effort, and our own edges—the movement available to us and how it feels. We’ll also discover how everyday activities and the spaces we inhabit can become opportunities to connect with the body. Even a small room offers possibilities.

What we’ll explore

  • ∿
    Attention from within

    Notice sensation, support, and breath, and explore how attention changes a familiar movement.

  • ∿
    Boundary exploration: getting to know the edge

    Investigate range, direction, and balance, with room to pause, adjust, and follow your curiosity.

  • ∿
    Making room for play

    Bring imagination into movement and discover how an ordinary task can become playful.

  • ∿
    The body in its habitat

    Explore how furniture, workspaces, and daily activities shape our movement—and what our bodies tell us about those arrangements.

  • ∿
    An Ayurvedic lens

    Consider appropriate effort, personal capacity, and the relationship between activity and well-being.

  • ∿
    Everyday integration

    Create a small personal exploration to carry into daily life.

Taking part

No previous experience with yoga, dance, or somatic practice is needed. Explorations can be approached seated, standing, or on the floor, at your own pace. There is space to rest, observe, and choose what feels appropriate.

Have a comfortable seat, a little clear space, a stable surface or wall nearby, and a cloth or small everyday object. You don’t need a large room or exercise equipment.

An invitation to become more familiar with your body—and more playful in the spaces you call home.
