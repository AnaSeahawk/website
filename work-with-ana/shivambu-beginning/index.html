Shivambu:Beginning

Beginning the relationship with the body’s own waters.

THE WORKSHOP

By video.
One to one: 90 minutes.
For a group: two hours, with open discussion.

There’s no right or wrong way to begin Shivambu. Some people start with a drop on the hand, some with a glassful, some by simply watching it in a glass jar for a few days. This workshop helps you find where you are, and ground into what Shivambu is and how long it has been around.

I bring my experience as a practitioner, and you bring your questions. Everyone is welcome, whether you want to practise or simply to understand.

What we’ll explore

  • ≋
    The name and its roots

    Shivambu, the water of Shiva, the Damar Tantra, and the first books most people meet: John Armstrong’s The Water of Life and Martha Christy’s Your Own Perfect Medicine.

  • ≋
    The body’s waters

    The kidneys, and the amniotic waters we began in.

  • ≋
    Ways to begin

    Your own comfort level, taste and food, first morning, midflow, and the sip cycle.

  • ≋
    Internal and external practice

    Where people start, and how far it can go.

  • ≋
    A personal journey

    Practising privately, and sharing with the people you live with when you’re ready.

  • ≋
    The moon cycle (menstruation)

    How practitioners meet the body when its rhythms change.

  • ≋
    Keeping a record

    A simple way to observe your own experience.

Taking part

No experience is needed, and nothing needs to be collected or prepared. Bring a notebook, something to write with, drinking water, and your questions.

Room for every question.
