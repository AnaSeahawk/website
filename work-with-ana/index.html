CONSULTATIONS

One to one, by video.
You bring the subject.

These private conversations offer space for the questions, observations and curiosity that grow around Shivambu (Urine Therapy), Sovereign Biophysics and alchemy. They draw on lived practice, sustained observation and years of independent study, from herbalism into Ayurveda.

Conversations can also explore fermentation, plants, a home that feels alive, or the relationship between nomadic and minimalist living—what we carry, what we need, and how we feel at home.

You bring the subject, and we find the way in together. Everything happens by video. For a home or a space, photos and a walk through it on camera are enough. I don’t need to be there.

Nomadic Living: At Home in the World

Making a life on the move begins with exploring what home means to you. For me, home is here, in my body, wherever I happen to be. Drawing on nomadic life with my partner since 2012, I offer a conversation grounded in lived experience—from preparing to leave and deciding what to do with your home and belongings, to choosing places, booking stays, managing moves, and finding your everyday rhythm. There’s also room for the personal side: homesickness, travelling as a couple, staying close to family, and being far away as their lives unfold. Whether you’re considering your first departure or already on the road, we can explore your questions and what it would take to make this way of living work for you.

Workshops

Every workshop I teach began with someone asking. They saw what I was doing and said, “Could you teach this?” I said yes, and we shaped it together.

One to one, any workshop is a 90-minute session: book a session and choose the workshop you’d like. For a group, a workshop runs two hours, the full 90 minutes and then half an hour of open discussion. Group workshops are arranged with me directly.
