Conversations

These private conversations offer space for the questions, observations and curiosity that grow around Shivambu, Sovereign Biophysics and alchemy. They draw on lived practice, sustained observation and years of independent study, from herbalism into Ayurveda.

Any of the subjects on this page can be a conversation: the body as home, Biophilia Living Design, soil and ferments, and the relationship between nomadic and minimalist living—what we carry, what we need, and how we feel at home.

You bring the subject, and we find the way in together. For a home or a space, photos and a walk through it on camera are enough. I don’t need to be there.

Nomadic Living: At Home in the World

Fifteen years of nomadic life, and home is here, in the body, wherever it happens to be. A conversation grounded in lived experience: leaving, belongings, choosing places and finding an everyday rhythm, and the personal side too: homesickness, travelling as a couple, family far away.

WORKSHOPS

One to one: 90 minutes.
For a group: two hours.

Workshops

Every workshop I teach began with someone asking. They saw what I was doing and said, “Could you teach this?” I said yes, and we shaped it together.

One to one, any workshop is a 90-minute session: book a session and choose the workshop you’d like. For a group, a workshop runs two hours, the full 90 minutes and then half an hour of open discussion. Group workshops are arranged with me directly.

Shivambu, in three parts

  • ≋
    Shivambu: Beginning

    Beginning the relationship with the body’s own waters: the name, the practice, menstruation, and room for every question. Read more →

  • ◉
    Shivambu: The Deeper Work

    For those already in the practice: the closed loop over years, fresh and aged waters, topical practice, and reading what the body is telling you.

  • ☿
    The Living Waters: Distillation and the Alchemical Roots

    The body as the first distillery. Mary the Jewess and the women of alchemy, the still and its three waters, and the names every tradition gave to what the body makes.

As above, so below

  • ☉
    The Living Year: Journal Like the Alchemists

    Learn to keep a journal the way the old alchemists did, by the Sun’s year and the cycles of the Moon. A practice complete on its own, and a first taste of the Living Year.

The Living Year membership is taking shape: a companion journal and a way to take part by the lunar cycle, the solar quarter or the full solar cycle. It isn’t open yet. Leave your email below to hear when it does.

The body as home

  • ∿
    Somatic Awareness: Attention from Within

    Explore movement, balance, and your own edges through curiosity and play—bringing body awareness into everyday life and the spaces you inhabit. Read more →

Urbaculture: home as habitat

  • ⌂
    Biophilia Living Design

    Make your home feel like you live in nature: light, decor, air quality, plants, and the living environment around you, so the space you live in becomes a habitat you take part in.

  • ◍
    Soil Makers

    Bokashi, the Phoenix Fly and humanure, working together as one system that turns all of a home’s waste into living soil, even in the city. Humanure is the old practice of returning our own manure to the earth. In the bokashi, the Phoenix Fly larvae work through it alongside the kitchen scraps and make it clean, rich soil.

  • ✺
    Eco Enzymes

    Fruit peels, sugar and water: a cleaner for the house, or a tonic and vinegar for the kitchen.
