CONSULTATIONS

One to one, by video.
You bring the subject.

People come to me with very different things. Some want their home to feel more alive. Some are herbalists, fermenters or students of alchemy who want to talk shop. Some have a question about their own practice with the body’s waters, or about the Sovereign Biophysics research. And some simply need a place to be heard.

Consultations happen by video. For a home or a space, photos and a walk through it on camera are enough. I don’t need to be there.

  • ⌂
    Biophilia Living Design

    Making your home a habitat you take part in: light, air, plants, materials and ambiance that help you feel at ease and connected to the living world around you. And the practical side, for homes and short-term rentals: shade over sun-facing glass, screens so doors can stay open, compost and recycling that are easy to keep.

  • ☿
    Alchemy and the herbal arts

    Plants as medicine and companions, fermentation, eco enzymes and soil (bokashi, humanure and the Phoenix Fly), and the alchemical tradition itself, from Women of Alchemy to the Golden Lexicon.

  • ≋
    The body’s own waters

    Shivambu as lived practice, and the Sovereign Biophysics work.

  • ☾
    A place to soften

    A question you have been turning over, a story you want to share, or a feeling that has not yet found its words.

Workshops

Every workshop I teach began with someone asking. They saw what I was doing and said, “Could you teach this?” I said yes, and we shaped it together.

Each workshop is an hour and a half of teaching, then half an hour for discussion and your questions. Groups stay small, so there is time for everyone. Dates are posted here as each workshop opens, and each person books their own seat.

Shivambu, in three parts

  • ≋
    Shivambu: Beginning

    Beginning the relationship with the body’s own waters: the name, the practice, where it starts, and room for every question.

  • ◉
    Shivambu: The Deeper Work

    For those already in the practice: the closed loop over years, fresh and aged waters, topical practice, and reading what the body is telling you.

  • ☿
    The Living Waters: Distillation and the Alchemical Roots

    The body as the first distillery. Mary the Jewess and the women of alchemy, the still and its three waters, and the names every tradition gave to what the body makes.

Urbaculture: home, kitchen and soil

  • ⌂
    Biophilia Living Design

    Make your home feel like you live in nature: light, decor, air quality, plants, and the living environment around you, so the space you live in becomes a habitat you take part in.

  • ◍
    Soil Makers

    Bokashi, the Phoenix Fly and humanure, working together as one system that turns all of a home’s waste into living soil, even in the city. Humanure is the old practice of returning our own manure to the earth. In the bokashi, the Phoenix Fly larvae work through it alongside the kitchen scraps and make it clean, rich soil.

  • ✺
    Eco Enzymes

    Fruit peels, sugar and water: a cleaner for the house, or a tonic and vinegar for the kitchen.

  • ◎
    Kefir and Cheese

    Living cultures at home, from the first grains to a fresh cheese.

As above, so below

  • ☉
    The Living Year: Journal Like the Alchemists

    Learn to keep a journal the way the old alchemists did, by the Sun’s year and the cycles of the Moon. A workshop on its own, or a first taste of the Living Year membership.
