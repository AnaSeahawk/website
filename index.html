Ana Seahawk

Living as Art

What matters most is the path of Heart.

Ana is an artist, writer and gatherer of people. Her habitat design work explores how we live with our surroundings—from the materials of everyday life to the arrangement and flow of a space.

◇

Across her art, writing and habitat design, Ana’s practice begins with close attention: a conversation, a sensation, a recurring image, a question that asks to be lived with. Making becomes a way of discovering what these encounters hold, giving form to something felt before it can be named.

There is a spirit of hospitality running through this work. Images, words and gatherings become places of encounter—openings through which something deeply personal can find recognition in another. Beauty invites attention; curiosity gives it somewhere to go.

Across its many forms, Ana’s practice returns to the exchange between private discovery and shared experience. Each work offers a place to pause, to notice, and to enter into a relationship of one’s own.

The Living Year

Returning to time as something we inhabit—through the changing light, the phases of the Moon, the turning seasons and the rhythms of our own lives.

The Living Year brings these cycles into daily attention. Its companion journal offers a place to pause, locate yourself beneath the moving sky, and record what is unfolding. Over time, the pages reveal what a single day cannot: patterns, returns, quiet changes and beginnings taking shape.

Join for a lunar cycle, explore a solar quarter, or journey through a full solar cycle. Each offers a different depth of attention to the year already unfolding around and within you.
