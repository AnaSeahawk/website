A living field

Returning to what was never absent.

The body holds its own mysteries, and its waters offer a doorway into deeper intimacy with oneself.

Ana Seahawk is a Shivambu (urine therapy) practitioner and Chartered Herbalist who carries these studies into Ayurveda, and her own practice into Sovereign Biophysics: writing and research that begin in the body and stay close to it.

Her inquiry reaches well beyond the body. Gold cast at a goldsmith’s bench, a house of her own design on an island bluff, hens and soil in Laos, fruit peels fermenting in a jar. One question runs through all of it: how to live within the living world, and what to bring into it.
