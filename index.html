Ana Seahawk

Living as Art

A living field

Returning to what was never absent.

Ana is a Shivambu (urine therapy) practitioner, independent researcher and explorer. Her foundation in herbalism has grown into a sustained study of Ayurveda. Her relationship with the body’s own waters has become the heart of Sovereign Biophysics, an evolving body of writing and research grounded in personal practice.

This inquiry grows from a life spent cultivating and creating: working with plants, food, water and the spaces we inhabit. Across these experiences runs a fascination with how we live, what we bring into our surroundings, and how we participate in the living world.
