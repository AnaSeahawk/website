WHAT WE CAN TALK ABOUT

Whatever the work
has opened for you.

People come to me with very different things. Some have a question about their own practice with the body’s waters, or about the Sovereign Biophysics research. Some want to make a home or a rental more ecological. Some want to talk about soil. And some simply need a place to be heard.

Everything happens by video. For a home or a space, photos and a walk through it on camera are enough. I don’t need to be there.

  • ⌂
    Ecological living and design

    Making a home or short-term rental cooler, easier and more ecological: shade over sun-facing glass, screens so doors can stay open, airflow, compost, recycling and eco enzymes set up so guests can keep them clean. More comfort, less air conditioning.

  • ◍
    Soil and the law of return

    Bokashi, humanure and the Phoenix Fly, in a city kitchen.

  • ≋
    The body’s own waters

    Urine therapy as lived practice, and the Sovereign Biophysics work.

  • ☾
    A place to soften

    A question you have been turning over, a story you want to share, or a feeling that has not yet found its words.
