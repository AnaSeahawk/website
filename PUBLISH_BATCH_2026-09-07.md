## Citations resolved before publication

The draft carried two blockers in its own source notes. Both are now settled, and
the resolution is recorded in the chapter.

**Damar Tantra, *Śivāmbu Kalpa Vidhi*.** Verses 6, 7 and 10 were checked against
the translation held at `Components/bibliography/urine-therapy/Damar-Tantra.epub`
and match the chapter's description of the early-morning intermediate stream and
the manner of receiving. The draft note said dating and textual history "remain to
be established before publication." They are not established, and the chapter now
says so plainly rather than implying a settled date — the verses are cited as what
one lineage recorded.

**Vinaya Piṭaka.** The draft asked for an exact passage location. *Pūtimuttabhesajja*
is the fourth of the four *nissāya* declared at ordination, **Mahāvagga I.30.4**:
"Medicine consisting of putrid urine is a trifling thing, easily obtained and
blameless."

Correction worth carrying: the bibliography companion note at
`Components/bibliography/buddhism/Vinaya-Pitaka-English.md` places
*pūtimuttabhesajja* in *Mahāvagga* VI (Bhesajjakkhandhaka). Mahāvagga VI is the
medicines chapter and at VI.14.7 permits a decoction of **cow's** urine for
jaundice — a related but distinct provision. The two should not be merged. That
companion note should be corrected separately.

The held scans could not confirm this directly: Müller SBE vols 13 and 17 have no
OCR text layer, and the Horner PDF is a poor scan of a different volume.
Verification was done against the canon and standard reference editions.
